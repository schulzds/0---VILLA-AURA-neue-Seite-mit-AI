Location

How to find your holiday home

Villa Aura is located in Kastraki on the west coast of Naxos — a quiet, spacious coastline known for long sandy beaches, crystal-clear water and breathtaking sunsets.

The beach is only a short walk away, while charming tavernas, cafés and small local spots are within easy reach. For groceries and daily essentials, you'll find supermarkets and bakeries just a few minutes by car.

Naxos offers the perfect balance of authentic island life and comfort: traditional villages, mountain views, hidden coves and a relaxed atmosphere — without feeling remote.
